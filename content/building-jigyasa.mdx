Jigyasa is a voice-first assistant that answers questions in Hindi over the MS MARCO-XI corpus, about 7.77 million passages. You speak, Sarvam STT transcribes, and the answer comes back grounded in retrieved text.

## Retrieval

Dense search alone missed exact-term matches, and BM25 alone missed paraphrases, so I combined them:

- FAISS HNSW for dense retrieval
- BM25 for sparse retrieval
- Reciprocal Rank Fusion to merge the two ranked lists
- A cross-encoder to rerank the fused results

I also wrote four length-routed chunking strategies, so short and long passages aren't chunked the same way.

## Knowing when not to answer

The assistant has an explicit abstention path for questions the corpus can't support. On 1,000 in-domain queries it produced 0 false positives, and across 16 off-topic or unsafe cases it produced 0 false negatives.

## Numbers

On 1,000 held-out queries: Recall@10 of 0.637, NDCG@10 of 0.465 and MRR of 0.259.

The local pipeline runs at P50/P70/P100 latencies of 114.0 / 122.6 / 142.5 ms. Dropping to K=10 cut latency by 44-48% with no quality loss I could observe.
